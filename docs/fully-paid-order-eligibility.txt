Development and isolated testing only. No production migration or deployment.

BEHAVIOR
Explicitly Admin-confirmed ADMIN_VERIFIED and LEGACY_RECONCILED funds now cover
all Items of an unambiguously fully paid order without creating allocations.
Both pickup scheduling and current Admin delivery recording consume the same
itemPaymentEligibility result, inside their existing authoritative transaction
and order locks. Order status, paymentState and displayed zero balance are not
payment evidence.

The shortcut requires safe integer amounts, unique Item/payment identifiers,
matching frozen order and Item due-now/future-balance totals, no payment adjustment,
valid allocation evidence, and enough independently verified funds to cover both
the frozen order total and the sum of active Item collection requirements. Stored
line amounts already include historical rounding and quantity; catalog prices and
historical unit-price recalculation are never used.

Previously allocated confirmed funds need no allocation top-up when the entire
order is covered. Existing valid allocations and reversals remain in the ledger;
they neither get duplicated nor become an additional payment requirement. Duplicate
spending still fails under the existing order lock, including concurrent requests.
FULL_ORDER/fundingSource describe derived coverage, not a new financial record.
Admin messaging distinguishes full-order coverage from an explicit allocation.

EXCEPTIONS / SAFEGUARDS
Existing Item-specific REQUIREMENT decisions retain their scheduling/collection
amounts and ADMIN_EXCEPTION source. A higher active collection requirement must
also be covered; full frozen-order payment cannot waive it. A lower exception or
gift does not lower the frozen-total trigger for this shortcut. On partially paid
orders, existing per-Item allocation and exception behavior remains in effect.

Opening credits are not automatically treated as received funds. They retain
explicit Admin allocation requirements. Pending receipts, assumed historical
funds, voids, duplicate identifiers, invalid allocations, inconsistent frozen totals
and unsupported evidence cannot establish full-order coverage. Mixed-order refunds
and adjustments retain their existing fail-closed treatment; known single-Item
adjustments retain their existing treatment. No refund attribution, free-text
exception conversion, reconciliation or historical backfill is introduced.

Full financial coverage does not bypass existing authentication, order status,
reservation, timing, blackout, capacity, membership or revision checks. Emergency
containment guards are unchanged. Eligibility evaluation creates no appointment,
payment, allocation, inventory or reservation update. Delivery still requires an
explicit Admin delivery action; eligibility never marks physical collection by
itself. David and Maria's final handover verification text and procedure remain.
